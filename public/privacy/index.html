Privacy Policy

This website is deliberately lean: no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising. If you choose the light or dark theme, only that setting is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never sent to me; you can delete it at any time in your browser settings. Personal data is only processed where this is technically necessary to run the site or when you contact me yourself.

For security reasons, this site uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by “https://” and the padlock symbol in your browser’s address bar.
